Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated & Effective: September 7, 2026 • Version 1.0.4

At ShieldBrowser Technologies, we stand behind the architectural precision and performance of our software. We believe in fair, transparent, and user-centric commercial practices.

Because ShieldBrowser is intangible, instantly activated digital software for macOS, this Cancellation & Refund Policy outlines our explicit guarantees, trial evaluation guidelines, eligibility criteria, and settlement procedures.

Key Customer Guarantee: If ShieldBrowser experiences an unresolved technical malfunction on your supported Mac that our engineers cannot fix within 24 hours of your report, you are entitled to a 100% full refund. Furthermore, because our passes are time-bound, there are zero recurring fees to cancel.

1. Complimentary 5-Minute Hardware Trial (Pre-Purchase Evaluation)

To ensure complete transparency and eliminate any risk of post-purchase dissatisfaction, every physical Mac computer receives a complimentary 5-minute hardware trial upon initial launch.

  • Zero Commitment: No credit card, bank details, or registration fees are required to access the trial.
  • Full Hardware Testing: The trial unlocks complete, unrestricted display-server isolation, Ghost Mode, and opacity controls.
  • Recommended Verification: We strongly advise every user to open an active test meeting on Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or Discord and confirm with a secondary test device that the browser window is 100% invisible prior to purchasing any paid pass.

2. Zero-Hassle Cancellation (Non-Recurring Passes)

ShieldBrowser does not utilize recurring subscription billing. All passes—24 Hours, 3 Days, 7 Days, 1 Month, 3 Months, and 1 Year—are time-bound digital passes that expire automatically at the conclusion of their epoch duration.

  • No Cancellation Forms Needed: Because there are no recurring subscriptions, you do not need to fill out cancellation requests, talk to retention agents, or disable recurring authorizations.
  • Zero Recurring Charges: Your account will never be charged without you manually choosing to purchase another pass inside the app.

3. Eligible Grounds for a 100% Full Refund

You are fully eligible for a 100% refund of your purchase under any of the following verified conditions:

  • Verifiable Technical Non-Performance: If ShieldBrowser fails to exclude its window from screen sharing on a supported Mac configuration (Apple Silicon M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M6 or supported Intel Core running macOS 12 to 27 Golden Gate) due to an OS-level conflict, and our engineering support desk is unable to resolve the issue within 24 hours of your support ticket.
  • Accidental Duplicate Charges: If a network latency spike, gateway timeout, or bank synchronization anomaly results in multiple charges for the same pass duration on the same machine.
  • Token Delivery Failure: If payment was successfully debited from your UPI or Card, but the application failed to cryptographically activate the pass and our engineering team was unable to manually provision it within 2 hours of contact.

4. Ineligible Circumstances

Refunds will not be issued under the following circumstances:

  • Completed Pass Consumption: Requests submitted after the purchased time-bound pass has already been substantially or fully utilized for its intended duration.
  • Unsupported or Modified Environments: Failure arising from running the software on unsupported non-macOS environments, Hackintosh configurations lacking Metal drivers, or virtual machines running unauthorized kernel modifications.
  • Terms Violations: Accounts suspended or terminated due to intentional reverse-engineering, binary cracking, or trial exploitation.
  • Third-Party Organizational Bans: Disciplinary actions imposed by third-party interview platforms or employers resulting from human visual discovery or physical camera observation.

5. Refund Request Process & Documentation

To request a refund, please contact our support team within 24 hours of purchase via email at support@shieldbrowser.com with the subject line Refund Request — [Your Transaction ID].

Please include the following information to expedite processing:

  • Your Payment Reference / Order ID (e.g., received via UPI/Card receipt).
  • The date and time of transaction.
  • Your Mac model (e.g., M3 Pro MacBook Pro) and macOS version.
  • A brief description or diagnostic screenshot of the technical malfunction encountered.

6. Settlement & Banking Timelines

Once your refund is approved by our billing team:

  • UPI Transactions (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED): Refunds are initiated immediately and typically credit back to your linked bank account within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Debit & Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Amex): Refunds are routed through your issuing bank and reflect on your statement within 5 to 7 business days, subject to standard banking clearing cycles.

All refunds are credited exclusively to the original payment method utilized during checkout. For security and anti-money laundering compliance, refunds cannot be redirected to third-party accounts.

7. Contact & Escalation Desk

If you have questions regarding a pending refund, billing discrepancy, or hardware transfer, please contact our dedicated billing desk:

  • Billing Support: support@shieldbrowser.com
  • Direct Support Portal: Contact Us & SLA Portal
  • Response Guarantee: Billing & refund tickets receive priority evaluation within 2 to 4 business hours.
