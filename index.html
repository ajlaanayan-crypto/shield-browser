Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Have questions about macOS window server exclusion, payment, or compatibility? Here are direct answers.

Will meeting attendees on Zoom or Google Meet see a black box where the window is?
No, absolutely not. Unlike DRM video players (such as Netflix or protected streaming software) that render an awkward, suspicious black cutout rectangle on screen shares, ShieldBrowser communicates directly with the native macOS Quartz Display Server compositor using CGWindowSharingType.none. The operating system's window server completely excludes ShieldBrowser's surface buffer from all frame-capture pipelines. Viewers in Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Discord, Slack, and OBS see only your natural desktop wallpaper or whatever software is running directly behind the window.
Does ShieldBrowser run natively on Apple Silicon M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, and M6 Macs?
Yes, 100% natively. ShieldBrowser is built as an optimized Apple Universal binary (arm64 + x86_64). It executes natively across the entire Apple Silicon lineup—including M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, and M6 chips (Base, Pro, Max, and Ultra configurations)—with direct Metal graphics acceleration and zero Rosetta 2 translation overhead. It is also fully verified on legacy Intel Core Macs running macOS 12.0 (Monterey) all the way up through the latest macOS 27.0+ (Golden Gate).
How does the 5-Minute Complimentary Hardware Trial work?
Every physical Mac receives an automatic, unrestricted 5-minute complimentary hardware test trial upon first launch. No credit card or registration fee is required. We strongly urge every user to open a test call on Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams, share their entire screen, and verify with a secondary device or test participant that ShieldBrowser is 100% invisible before purchasing any time-bound pass.
How does in-app pass activation work, and are there any recurring subscriptions?
All pass purchases take place securely and natively inside the ShieldBrowser macOS application. There are zero recurring subscription charges and zero hidden auto-renewal traps. When your pass expires, it simply ends without unexpected credit card debits. You can choose from 6 transparent, time-bound passes (24 Hours, 3 Days, 7 Days, 1 Month, 3 Months, or 1 Year) and pay seamlessly via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED) or Debit/Credit Cards. Your pass activates cryptographically within 5 seconds.
What is Ghost Mode, and why is the 36% Opacity Sweet Spot recommended?
Pressing ⌘ + Shift + H activates Ghost Mode, which strips away the window chrome, address bar, and tabs to provide a 100% borderless floating web viewport. By pressing ⌘ + ⌥ + 6, you lock the browser at exactly 36% opacity—the scientifically calibrated "Stealth Sweet Spot." At 36%, web text, documentation, and interview notes remain crisp and readable to you, while your background desktop or coding IDE shines through naturally, making your gaze and screen activity appear completely organic.
How does Click-Through Mode work while coding or taking notes?
Pressing ⌘ + Shift + C toggles Click-Through Mode. When enabled, your mouse cursor passes completely through the ShieldBrowser window as if it were air, allowing you to click, highlight, scroll, and type in VS Code, Xcode, Figma, or your Terminal running underneath—all while keeping your reference notes floating visibly on top! Pressing Escape (Esc) instantly restores normal cursor interaction to ShieldBrowser.
Can I transfer my pass if I change or upgrade my Mac?
Yes. Each pass is cryptographically anchored to your physical Mac's hardware identifier (IOPlatformUUID) to prevent unauthorized credential sharing and key piracy. If you purchase a new MacBook, iMac, or Mac Studio, simply reach out to our dedicated support desk at support@shieldbrowser.com with your transaction reference. Our engineering team will release the old hardware token and re-bind your active pass to your new machine in under 2 hours.
Does ShieldBrowser record or store my web browsing history or credentials?
Never. ShieldBrowser operates on a strict Zero-Logs Architecture. We do not operate remote telemetry collectors, behavioral trackers, or keyloggers. Your cookies, browsing history, cache, and saved credentials reside exclusively inside your Mac's encrypted sandboxed container on your local APFS disk. Furthermore, our native Apple WebKit engine uses isolated memory pools that are purged immediately upon quitting the application.
What is the Cancellation and Refund Policy?
We stand behind the engineering excellence of ShieldBrowser. If you encounter an unresolved technical malfunction or compatibility failure on any supported macOS version (macOS 12 to 27 Golden Gate) that our engineering team cannot fix within 24 hours of report, you are entitled to a 100% full refund. Because passes are time-bound and non-recurring, there are no cancellation fees or lock-ins. Full details are available on our Refund Policy page.
How do I open the .DMG if Apple Gatekeeper shows a security prompt?
Because ShieldBrowser is distributed independently outside the Mac App Store to support advanced display-server exclusion APIs, macOS Gatekeeper may prompt: "Apple cannot check it for malicious software." To open it smoothly: right-click (or Control-click) ShieldBrowser in your Applications folder and select Open, then click Open in the dialog. Alternatively, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway, or run xattr -cr /Applications/ShieldBrowser.app in Terminal.
