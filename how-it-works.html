Display Compositor Architecture

The Science of True Invisibility

Why traditional DRM hacks create suspicious black boxes, and why Apple Quartz Compositor isolation leaves zero footprint.

❌ Standard Browsers & DRM Hacks

1️⃣ Standard Chrome / Safari window renders on your screen.
↓
2️⃣ Meeting app requests full screen buffer via ScreenCaptureKit.
↓
3️⃣ Window is included in the frame OR DRM blocks it with an obvious black rectangle.
↓
🚨 Result: Interviewers immediately see your secret tabs or a suspicious black cutout!

✅ ShieldBrowser Quartz Compositor Isolation

1️⃣ ShieldBrowser sets CGWindowSharingType.none on its window buffer.
↓
2️⃣ Meeting app requests screen stream from Apple Quartz Window Server.
↓
3️⃣ Quartz skips ShieldBrowser entirely and renders only underlying windows.
↓
✨ Result: Viewers see a clean desktop with VS Code/Terminal. Zero detection, zero black boxes!
