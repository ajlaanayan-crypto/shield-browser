Fulfillment & Provisioning Policy

Digital Goods Delivery Policy

Last Updated & Effective: September 7, 2026 • Version 1.0.4

This Digital Goods Delivery Policy describes how software applications, digital licenses, and time-bound passes purchased from ShieldBrowser Technologies are fulfilled and delivered to customers.

Summary: ShieldBrowser is exclusively downloadable software. We do not dispatch physical boxes or shipments. All pass purchases are fulfilled electronically and activated natively inside the macOS application within 0 to 5 seconds.

1. Exclusively Digital Software Products (No Physical Shipments)

All products offered by ShieldBrowser Technologies—including the macOS application bundle, installer image (ShieldBrowser-v1.0.4.dmg), and time-bound digital passes (24-Hour, 3-Day, 7-Day, 1-Month, 3-Month, and 1-Year)—are 100% digital, intangible electronic products.

  • There are zero physical shipments, parcels, postal boxes, USB drives, or physical installation media sent to any postal or shipping address.
  • Shipping and postal handling charges do not apply to any transactions on ShieldBrowser.
  • The price shown during checkout is the complete, final amount for the digital pass.

2. Instant In-App Provisioning Protocol (< 5 Seconds)

All pass activations occur natively inside the ShieldBrowser macOS application using an automated cryptographic provisioning pipeline:

  • Step 1 (In-App Checkout): You select your preferred time-bound pass in the app and complete checkout via native UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED) or Debit/Credit Cards.
  • Step 2 (Instant Gateway Webhook): Upon payment capture, our secure payment gateway issues an automated server-to-server cryptographic confirmation.
  • Step 3 (Hardware Token Unlock): The licensing server generates a cryptographically signed token bound to your Mac's IOPlatformUUID.
  • Step 4 (Immediate Interface Unlock): Within 0 to 5 seconds of payment authorization, the browser UI unlocks full browsing capabilities and initializes the real-time epoch countdown timer reflecting your selected pass duration. No application restarts are required.

3. Invoicing & Proof of Delivery

Concurrently with the in-app hardware token delivery:

  • An electronic receipt and detailed tax invoice containing your unique Transaction Reference Number, payment timestamp, and pass duration is automatically delivered to your registered email address.
  • This electronic confirmation serves as your legal proof of delivery and software license entitlement.

4. Offline Operation Capability

Once your pass is cryptographically bound to your physical Mac:

  • The active license token is cached securely within your Mac's sandboxed keychain/container.
  • You do not need a continuous, persistent connection to our licensing servers to maintain browsing; the browser validates your active token against your local system clock and hardware UUID.

5. Re-Download & Installer Replacement Rights

You retain the right to re-download the official ShieldBrowser-v1.0.4.dmg installer at any time at zero additional charge from:

  • Primary Hub: shieldbrowser.com/download
  • Verified Mirror: Official MediaFire Mirror

If you delete the app or format your machine during an active pass duration, simply re-download the DMG, drag ShieldBrowser to Applications, and click Sync License in the top menu to re-validate your active pass.

6. Delivery Failure Assistance (< 2 Hours Resolution SLA)

In the rare circumstance that a network timeout, cellular glitch, or banking latency debits your account without immediately unlocking the in-app countdown timer:

  • Self-Service Sync: Click the Sync License button located in the browser's top-right settings dropdown. The client will query the gateway and immediately apply the pending pass token.
  • Priority Support Ticket: If the pass remains locked, forward your transaction reference to our engineering escalation desk at support@shieldbrowser.com.
  • Guaranteed SLA: Under our Priority 1 Delivery SLA, our engineering team will manually reconcile the transaction with the payment gateway and provision your pass within 2 business hours, or issue a 100% full refund at your discretion.

7. Hardware Upgrade & License Re-Binding

Because passes are cryptographically bound to your physical Mac's hardware identifier (IOPlatformUUID), passes cannot be used concurrently across multiple machines. If you upgrade to a new MacBook, iMac, or Mac Studio:

  • Email support@shieldbrowser.com with your original order reference and the new Mac's hardware identifier (displayed in ShieldBrowser > About).
  • Our team will revoke the legacy hardware token and re-bind your remaining pass duration to your new machine at zero cost.

8. Contact & Delivery Inquiries

For any delivery, pass synchronization, or invoice inquiries:

  • Delivery Support Email: support@shieldbrowser.com
  • Customer Portal: Contact Us & SLA Portal
  • Operating Entity: ShieldBrowser Technologies
